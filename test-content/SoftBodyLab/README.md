# SoftBodyLab

Engine test content for cloth and destruction (`docs/SOFTBODY.md`). Not a demo game, and no third-party assets:
every mesh is generated by `make_soft_lab.py` and every `.ocsoft` is a few lines of JSON.

| Placement | Component | What to try |
|---|---|---|
| Flag | `Cloth` cloth_light | Wind: Environment > "Copy to cloth wind", or the Set Soft Body Wind node. It hangs from its pole edge. |
| Curtain | `Cloth` cloth_heavy | Walk into it, drop a rigid body on it. |
| Cape (on the mannequin) | `Cloth` cloth_heavy, `tearable=1` | Tear it with `Tear Soft Body`; the part with no pin falls as a piece. `Release Soft Body` drops it whole. |
| GlassPane | `Destructible` glass | Throw something at it: it cracks from the hit and falls apart in pieces. |
| SteelDoor | `Destructible` metal | Hit it hard: it dents (permanent set) and holds on its hinge edge. |
| PlankCrate | `Destructible` wood | Spawns 3 m up and drops: it breaks into planks on landing. |
| HighPolyDoor | `Destructible` metal, no asset | A 50 x 120 quad sheet (12k particles, over the 4096 cap). Without a proxy the build is refused and it stays a rigid door; with one it simulates the proxy and the full mesh follows. See "Proxy" below. |

Wave 10 adds to the three breakables: thick walls and a back skin on every break (`caps`), tear/detach/dust effects with
sounds (`effects`), and a damage that survives a save. The cap interior material is left empty (one material); see
"Interior material".

## Setting it up

1. `python make_soft_lab.py` (writes `Content/Source/*.obj`; run it once to get `HighPolyDoor.obj`, the other OBJ files
   are already checked in). `python make_soft_lab_audio.py` rewrites the four break sounds under `Content/Audio/`
   (`Thud`, `WoodCrack`, `GlassBreak`, `MetalClang`: procedural and deterministic, test content only).
2. Import the OBJ files so `Content/Meshes/*.ocmesh` exist (Content Browser > Import, or `AverAssetC` on each
   `.obj` with `--out-dir Content/Meshes`). The level names `Meshes/Flag.ocmesh` and so on; until they exist
   the placements draw nothing.
3. Open `Maps/SoftBodyLab.ocworld` and press Play. Soft bodies only simulate in Play.

## Effects

The `.ocsoft` effects name sounds that exist (`Audio/*.wav`) and particle paths (`Particles/GlassShards.ocparticle`,
`GlassDust.ocparticle`, and so on) that are placeholders: the effects themselves belong to the GPU particle module. Until
those assets exist the sounds play and the particles are skipped with one logged warning.

## Interior material

A cap wall shows the material's interior only when `caps.interiorMaterial` names one. No such material is checked in, so the
lab leaves it empty and the walls use the outer material. To try it: make a dull-grey or splintered-wood material, put its
path in Details > Destructible > Caps > Interior material, and break the crate again.

## Proxy

1. Import `HighPolyDoor.obj` (step 2).
2. Select HighPolyDoor, Details > Destructible > Proxy > Cook proxy (the sandbox Proxy tool welds, decimates to roughly 600
   triangles and writes `Meshes/HighPolyDoor_proxy.ocmesh`, then sets the asset's `proxy.mesh`).
3. Play and hit it. The proxy tears; the full mesh follows. Details shows the proxy bit and the particle count.

## Tuning

`Maps/SoftBodyTune.ocworld` is the preset rig: a cloth_light flag, a cloth_heavy curtain, a metal door, a glass pane and a
wood crate in a row, with a wind volume over the cloths. Turn on Show > Soft Bodies > Tuning, select a target and use the
Hit buttons (5, 25, 90, 400, 3000 J, Repair between). The HUD prints the deposit, dent depth, broken beams and strain.
`SoftBodyCalibrationTest` prints the same ladder headless; keep both tables with the numbers you change. The intended
classes: metal dents at 90 J and holds; glass does nothing at 1 J and makes four or more pieces at 90 J; wood splits along
the grain at about 400 J; the light flag moves in a 300 cm/s wind and never stretches past 10 percent.

## Limits of this level

- The cape hangs from the mannequin entity (one joint), not from a skeleton. A cape skinned to a character's
  spine needs a skinned mesh and a `bone` rule in its `.ocsoft`; there is none here because that needs authored
  skin weights.
- The numbers in the `.ocsoft` files and the presets are starting values (UNVERIFIED) until the headed tuning
  pass.
- `SoftBodyTune` has no Hit graph: the Hit buttons in Details do the same job. A graph that fires `Soft Body Damage` on a
  key press would need a `.ocgraph`, which this directory does not carry.
- The wave 10 content (caps, effects, proxy, the Tune map, the audio) has not been opened in the editor yet.
