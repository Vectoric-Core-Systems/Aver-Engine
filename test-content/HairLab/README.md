# HairLab

Engine test content for strand hair (`docs/HAIR.md`, design `docs/plans/wave11/SOFTBODY_HAIR.md`). Not a demo game, and no third-party assets: the
head and the scalp are generated by `make_hair_lab.py`, and each groom is a few lines of text (the HEAD records of an `.ochair`) that
`AverAssetC hair-text` turns into the binary asset. A groom with a `GENERATE` record and no strands is regenerated from its scalp when it loads,
so the strands themselves are never checked in.

| Placement | Groom | What to look at |
|---|---|---|
| HeadShortStraight | `Hair/ShortStraight.ochair` (6000 strands, 6 to 9 cm) | The shading: the white R highlight and the coloured TRT one move along the strands as you orbit; tips lighter than roots; no gap between scalp and roots. |
| HeadLongCurly | `Hair/LongCurly.ochair` (9000 strands, 24 to 34 cm, curl, 150 clumps) | Motion and the strand-count levels: walk away and the strands thin and fatten without a pop; the curl keeps its shape when the head turns. |
| HeadPonytail | `Hair/Ponytail.ochair` (8000 strands, 20 to 28 cm, pulled back) | Collision and swing: the strands should not sink into the skull. The heads are rigid, so there are no neck or shoulder proxies; the default humanoid set needs a rig (see "Motion"). |

## Setting it up

1. `python make_hair_lab.py` (writes `Content/Source/HairHead.obj`, `HairScalp.obj`, `Content/Hair/*.hair.txt`, `Content/Maps/HairLab.ocworld` and
   `Game.ocproject`; deterministic).
2. Import the two OBJ files so `Content/Meshes/HairHead.ocmesh` and `HairScalp.ocmesh` exist (Content Browser > Import, or
   `AverAssetC convert Content/Source/HairHead.obj --out-dir Content/Meshes` and the same for `HairScalp`). The scalp mesh is the cap of a sphere a
   little larger than the head: the roots grow from its triangles and nothing grows on the face.
3. Write the grooms:

   ```
   AverAssetC hair-text Content/Hair/ShortStraight.hair.txt --out Content/Hair/ShortStraight.ochair
   AverAssetC hair-text Content/Hair/LongCurly.hair.txt     --out Content/Hair/LongCurly.ochair
   AverAssetC hair-text Content/Hair/Ponytail.hair.txt      --out Content/Hair/Ponytail.ochair
   ```

   Edit a `.hair.txt` and run the line again to change a groom.
4. Open `Maps/HairLab.ocworld` and press Play. Whether the heads also show their hair in the editor viewport outside Play follows where the host calls
   `tickSoftBodies` (SandboxUpdate.cpp); that is worth checking first.

## Checks (docs/plans/wave11/SOFTBODY_HAIR.md section 6, items 6 to 9)

* **Look.** Orbit each head in sun and in shade. No gap between scalp and roots; ribbons read as strands, not tape; no black hair when backlit
  (the 0.15 floor stands in for multiple scattering); no flicker at rest with TAA on. With TAA off the thin strands show as a dither (expected).
* **Motion.** The heads are rigid and static. To see inertia, select a head in Play and move it (or parent it to something that moves): the hair lags
  and settles, and a move of more than `teleportCm` (200 cm by default) restarts it from its default pose instead of whipping.
* **Wind.** `Set Hair Wind` (a `Hair` node) or `Hair.SetWind` from a script bends the tips more than the roots; `Wind scale` and `Feels wind` on the component change it per character.
* **Levels of detail.** Walk away along +X: at 300 cm, 700 cm and 1500 cm the groom thins to 50%, 25% and 12% of its strands with fatter ribbons, with a
  15% hysteresis. Beyond 2500 cm the simulation stops (Details shows "frozen") and resumes without a snap.
* **Budget.** Statistics: the draw buffers of one 9000-strand groom are about 38 MB the first time and 23 MB for each further instance of the same
  groom (arithmetic in the design, 2.21); the first build of the 9000-strand groom stalls a frame (UNVERIFIED: 0.2 to 1 s).

## Motion with a rig

The proxies (head sphere, neck and shoulder capsules, spine box) need a skeleton: put a `Hair` component on a character that has a `CSkeletalMesh`, list
its joints in `SCALPJOINTS` (for example `SCALPJOINTS Head`), and give it a scalp mesh skinned to that rig (`scalpFromMesh` maps the mesh's bones to
the joints by name). `AverAssetC hair <file.gltf> --scalp <mesh.ocmesh> --joints Head` does the same for curves authored elsewhere.

## Checks for phases I to K (docs/HAIR.md 4i)

Run D3D12 first, then the same list with `--backend vulkan`, with the owner's `editor.ini` and never over a running editor.

* **HairSelfTest** (`HairSelfTest`, then `HairSelfTest vulkan`): the CPU backend against the GPU host, 120 steps, with and without wind, within 0.2 cm. Exit 77 means no device.
* **Groom tab.** Content Browser > New Hair Groom (or double-click `Hair/Ponytail.ochair`). Name the scalp `Meshes/HairScalp.ocmesh` and watch the preview grow
  roots on it; drag Strands, Length and Curl (the preview follows within about a tenth of a second, and one drag is one undo step); add a material and a
  look, change a gradient, switch the three views, add the humanoid proxy set, Save, close and reopen: everything is back. "Import curves" on a glTF with
  line strands in the scalp's space replaces the stored strands (not an undo step).
* **Overlay.** Show > Hair with the Ponytail head selected: amber guide strands at rest, no proxies on a rigid head. On a rigged character: cyan hulls on the
  head, neck and shoulders that follow the animation.
* **Script and nodes.** `Entity.AddHair`, `SetHairWidthScale`, `AddHairCollider(Sphere, (6,0,0), "Hand_R")` and `TeleportHair` from a script, or the `Hair` node
  family in a graph; `TryGetHair` / `Get Hair` report the level and the strand counts. `Set Hair Look` with an `.ocmat` whose shading model is hair changes the colour.
* **Shadows and ray tracing.** By default the hair is absent from RT shadows and reflections (turn RT shadows on and look); tick `Ray traced` on the Hair
  component and it appears, and `--gpu-timing` shows what it costs (region `Hair` plus the Voxi acceleration-structure build). Untick `Casts shadows`
  and the hair's shadow-map shadow goes. GI: the hair adds no indirect light either way (a compute-written mesh is never voxelised).
* **TAA.** At rest no flicker with TAA on; a fast head turn may ghost a little, like a skinned character. With TAA off the thin strands dither (expected).
* **Budgets.** `Stats`: the first 9000-strand instance about 38 MB, each further instance of the same groom about 23 MB; the first build stalls one frame
  (UNVERIFIED, 0.2 to 1 s). A groom over the 96 MB hair budget is refused with the number in the log.
* **Vulkan.** Everything above identical in character. If the hair simulates wrongly only on Vulkan, suspect the structured-buffer layout
  (`-fvk-use-dx-layout` needs `scalarBlockLayout`) or the b3 / b4 constants (docs/HAIR.md 4h); the Voxi-on-Vulkan baseline defect (parity plan item V)
  limits what can be judged of the lighting.
